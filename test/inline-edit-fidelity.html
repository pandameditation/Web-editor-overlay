Case study

  • Overview
  • Process
  • Directional survey. With no internal research budget, I secured $100 of donated PickFu credit and used it to reach 30 US travelers. After reviewing the protocol with PM and UX team, we asked five forced-choice statements and one open question: what could an airline AI chatbot do that would make someone angry enough to quit? After analyzing the results, I was able to present every finding with Engineering, Product Management and the wider project team in the same week!
  • Alignment workshop. In a remote session with Engineering and Product, we mapped the failure modes onto the existing flow and agreed which ones the assistant had to refuse outright.
  • Prototype rounds. Three rounds of clickable prototypes, each one narrowing the set of questions the assistant would answer without a human in the loop.
